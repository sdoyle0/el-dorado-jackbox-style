Map builder

Add piece faces, drag them onto the board, rotate in 60° steps, and snap their anchors to the hex grid. Red dashed edges are joins between different pieces.

Diagram overlay

Upload a crop of the setup diagram, then drag the image to line it up under the hexes. Use opacity and width to compare terrain and piece outlines.

Placed pieces

Select a placed piece on the board or in the list. Drag it to move; it snaps to the hex grid.

Import layout

Export
