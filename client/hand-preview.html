How the rare big hands work

Normal turn = 4 cards. Cartographer draws +2 (→ ~5–6 in hand), Compass +3 (→ ~6), Scientist +1 / Travel Log +2 then you remove cards so it shrinks back to 4 at End Turn. Only chained draws (play Cartographer, then Compass from the new cards) reach 7–8 — rare and temporary. A scrolls a little; B′ is already 3-across so 6 cards are exactly 2 rows and 8 cards add one more row. In both, Market / End Turn / Discard stay pinned below while the grid scrolls.
